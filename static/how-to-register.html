Registration Process

Follow these simple steps to complete your registration for the Spheronix Hackathon 2026.

Mandatory Nodal Officer Responsibilities

Please note that individual student registrations are NOT permitted. As the Nodal Officer or Principal, you are fully responsible for the following:

  • Collecting the details of all participating students from your institution.
  • Filling out and uploading the consolidated bulk Excel sheet.
  • Collecting fees from the students and making a single, consolidated payment to the platform.
1

Open the Registration Page

On the Hackathon home page, click “Register as Nodal / Principal”.

2

Enter Institution Details

Fill in all required institution details, including:

  • State
  • District
  • Pincode
  • College Code
  • College Name
  • Affiliated To
  • Institution Type

After entering all the required information, click “Next Phase”.

3

Enter Principal & Nodal Officer Details

Select the appropriate Registration Category and provide:

  • Principal Name
  • Principal Gmail Address
  • Principal Mobile Number
  • Nodal Officer Name
  • Nodal Officer Gmail Address
  • Nodal Officer Mobile Number

Verify the Principal's email address if required, then click “Submit Registration”.

4

Registration Confirmation

After successful submission, a “Registration Successful” message will be displayed. Return to the home page using “Return Home”.

5

Login to the Dashboard

From the home page, click “Login as Nodal / Principal”.

Enter your:

  • User ID
  • Password

Then click “Login”.

6

Login with credentials

For enhanced account security:

  1. Loign with admin given credentials
  2. User ID
  3. Password
7

Complete 2FA Verification

On subsequent login, enter the 6-digit code generated by your authenticator app and click “Verify” to access the dashboard.

8

Enter otp from 2FA Verification(authenticator)

In the dashboard:

  1. It diplay 6 digits code .
  2. Copy the code 6 digit code paste the code in input field.
  3. After enter the code click on verify.
9

Review Student Data

As the Nodal Officer, you must upload all your students at once.

  1. Download the Sample Excel template.
  2. Enter the details of ALL participating students.
  3. Save the file in CSV, XLSX, or XLS format.
  4. Upload the completed file under “Upload Data File”.
  5. Click “Process File” and review the uploaded student details carefully.

Important: You are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the student data before submitting.

10

Complete Fee Payment

As the Nodal Officer, you must collect the fees from your students and make a consolidated payment.

  • The system will automatically calculate the total amount (e.g., 10 students * 399 = ₹3990).
  • Choose your preferred payment method (QR Code or Bank Transfer).
  • Make the total consolidated payment from your account or the institution's account.
11

Upload Payment Proof

After completing the payment:

  1. Upload the payment screenshot under “Payment Proof & Final Submission”.
  2. Review all uploaded information carefully.
  3. Tick the confirmation checkbox stating that the payment has been made successfully and the uploaded details are correct.
  4. Click “Submit Application”.
12

Submission Locked

Once the application is submitted successfully, the application will be locked and cannot be modified.

Important: Please verify all institution details, student data, and payment information carefully before clicking “Submit Application.”
